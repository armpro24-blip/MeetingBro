# MeetingBro Streaming-Whisper Preview Lane Benchmark

> Generated by `scripts/benchmark_latency_accuracy_frontier.py --streaming-preview`.
> Measures the `whisper-streaming` (LocalAgreement-2) preview lane vs the formal Whisper lane
> on the real AMI meeting fixture (`ami_en2002a`, ~120 s single-distant-mic conversational English).

## Configuration

- fixture: `ami_en2002a` (en, AMI EN2002a [300-420s], CC BY 4.0)
- Whisper: `small` / `cpu` / `int8` / beam `3`
- knob point: pre_vad_max_seconds=4  asr_accumulation_seconds=2.0
- streaming lane: `StreamingWhisperAdapter` (LocalAgreement-2, shared formal model, no second load)
- latency definition: audio-end → segment/preview event emitted (realtime replay)

## Interpretation vs Success Criteria

### Criterion 1: Committed-caption p50 well under ~3.8 s formal median (target ~1–1.5 s)
- **MET** — streaming p50 = 1.48 s  (formal p50 = 3.94 s; speedup = 2.7×)

### Criterion 2: ASR RTF < 1 (no sustained backpressure)
- **MET** — streaming preview RTF = 0.62 (< 1.0)

## Notes

- The formal lane is unchanged and remains the authoritative transcript source.
- The streaming lane uses the **same loaded model** (no second WhisperModel); decode overlap with the formal lane on a single-core CPU may inflate RTF under heavy load.
- Latency is measured as `wall-clock_now − audio_end_time`; preview events fire on committed words, so p50 reflects when LocalAgreement-2 first stabilises a prefix — typically after 2 growing-window steps.
- AMI SDM (single-distant-mic) is challenging: overlapping speakers and far-field audio inflate WER. Latency numbers are the relevant metric for this lane.
